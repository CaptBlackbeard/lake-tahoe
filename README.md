# Tahoe, the Long Way Up

A Christmas road-trip plan from Lancaster, CA up US-395 to Lake Tahoe, Thursday 10 to Sunday 13 December 2026, for two adults and a two-year-old.

**Live site:** https://captblackbeard.github.io/lake-tahoe/
**Map:** https://captblackbeard.github.io/lake-tahoe/map/

## What's on the site

- Will there be snow? Historical snow-on-the-ground odds and temperatures for 11-13 December from NOAA station records, plus a live National Weather Service forecast that switches on about a week before the trip
- A North Shore / South Shore switch: each base has its own Friday-to-Sunday plan, hotels, Christmas events, food, coffee and wine
- Twenty-one stops up US-395 from Jawbone Canyon to Reno, roughly every half hour, each checked for December opening
- A night in Reno with a Marriott-family hotel, Basque dinner and a children's museum
- Marriott hotels at the lake (Ritz-Carlton Lake Tahoe, Marriott Grand Residence Club) plus whimsical alternatives
- Christmas events dated for that weekend: Heavenly Village tree lighting, Festival of Winter Lights, Breakfast with Santa, sleigh rides
- Winter driving: chain controls, daylight, gas and cell gaps
- Interactive map with the road route, numbered stops and every place on the page

## Notes

- The site is static HTML served by GitHub Pages; there is no build step in this repo.
- Pages are marked `noindex`, so search engines are asked not to list them.
- Photo credits and sources are listed at the bottom of the page.
- Hero: Albert Bierstadt, *Lake Tahoe in Winter* (public domain).
- Music: a Santa playlist that starts on a random song, plays the rest in turn and has a Next button. *Up on the Housetop* and *Yes, Virginia, There Is a Santa Claus* (United States Air Force bands, public domain); *Jolly Old St. Nicholas* by Steve Combs (CC BY 4.0); *Santa Claus Is Coming to Town* by John Bartmann (recording CC BY 4.0; the 1934 song is still under copyright). All via Wikimedia Commons.
